Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

LottoManager does not collect any personal data. There is no LottoManager account, and the app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no third-party code that could do any of these.

Your syndicate data

Everything you enter stays with you: members, payments, tickets, results, prizes and agreements, including signatures. It is stored on your devices. If iCloud is turned on, it syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. The developer cannot see it.

Some of this data is about other people, such as the names of your syndicate's members. It is kept the same way, on your devices and in your iCloud, and is never sent to the developer.

To remove your data, delete it in the app, or delete the app and its data in your iCloud settings.

Camera

If you scan a paper copy of a syndicate agreement, the app uses the camera to turn the pages into a PDF on your device. The camera is not used for anything else. The PDF is stored with your syndicate and only leaves your device through your own iCloud sync.

Game rules

The app downloads a public file of lottery game rules (prices, draw days and prizes) from GitHub, where it is hosted. The request carries nothing about you or your syndicate. As with any website, GitHub receives the network address the request comes from.

Apple

Apple handles subscriptions through the App Store. Depending on your own device settings, your device may also share crash and diagnostic reports with Apple. Apple's privacy policy covers these. LottoManager does not start, access or control them.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, but GitHub may log the network address of visitors, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
